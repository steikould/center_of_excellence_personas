# Exercise: AI Stakeholder Analysis

> **For: Senior Director, Project Manager, Enterprise Architect**

## Instructions

Use Copilot to build a stakeholder analysis for an AI initiative at your animal pharmaceutical company.

### Step 1 — Identify Stakeholders
Ask Copilot:
```
I'm launching an AI initiative at an animal pharmaceutical company. The initiative is: [describe your AI project — e.g., "predictive quality for biologics manufacturing" or "automated adverse event detection"].

Identify all stakeholders who would be affected by or have influence over this initiative. Categorize them by: Executive, Functional Leader, Technical, Regulatory, External.
```

### Step 2 — Map Influence & Interest
Ask Copilot to create a 2x2 influence/interest grid:
```
For each stakeholder you identified, assess their:
- Influence (High/Low): Can they block or accelerate this initiative?
- Interest (High/Low): How much do they care about the outcome?

Present as a table with recommended engagement strategy for each quadrant:
- High Influence + High Interest → Manage Closely
- High Influence + Low Interest → Keep Satisfied
- Low Influence + High Interest → Keep Informed
- Low Influence + Low Interest → Monitor
```

### Step 3 — Communication Plan
Ask Copilot to draft a communication plan:
```
For the "Manage Closely" stakeholders, create a communication plan:
- What information they need
- How often they need updates
- What format (executive brief, dashboard, detailed report)
- What decisions they need to make and when
- What concerns they're likely to have (especially around regulatory compliance, data privacy, and AI governance)
```

### Step 4 — Save
Save the complete stakeholder analysis to:
`personas/{your-role}/workspace/artifacts/stakeholder-analysis-{initiative-name}.md`

## Success Criteria
- At least 10 stakeholders identified across categories
- Influence/interest assessment is realistic
- Communication plan addresses regulatory and compliance concerns
- Animal pharma context is specific (not generic "healthcare")
