# Exercise: AI Governance Readiness Checklist

> **For: Senior Director, Enterprise Architect, Project Manager**

## Instructions

Use Copilot to assess your organization's AI governance readiness.

### Step 1 — Self-Assessment
Ask Copilot:
```
I need to assess our animal pharmaceutical company's readiness for AI governance. Walk me through a checklist covering these domains:

1. Policy & Standards — Do we have AI usage policies? Data classification? Model risk framework?
2. Organization — Is there a governance board? Clear roles and responsibilities? Escalation paths?
3. Technology — Do we have model registries? Experiment tracking? Automated monitoring?
4. Process — Do we have change control for AI? Validation protocols? Incident response?
5. People — Do we have AI literacy programs? Ethics training? Role-specific AI skills?
6. Compliance — Are we addressing 21 CFR Part 11? ALCOA+? GxP validation for AI?

For each item, I'll rate us as: Not Started | In Progress | Implemented | Mature.
Present as an interactive checklist.
```

### Step 2 — Gap Prioritization
```
Based on my assessment, prioritize the gaps:
- Critical: Must address before any production AI deployment
- High: Should address within 6 months
- Medium: Address within 12 months
- Low: Nice to have / continuous improvement

Consider our animal pharmaceutical regulatory context — what would an FDA inspector or auditor expect to see?
```

### Step 3 — Action Plan
```
For the Critical and High priority gaps, create an action plan:
- Specific deliverable needed
- Recommended owner (by persona/role)
- Dependencies
- Estimated effort
- Connection to knowledge base artifacts (which module/persona addresses this)
```

### Step 4 — Save
Save to: `personas/{your-role}/workspace/artifacts/governance-readiness.md`

Also consider contributing findings to: `knowledge-base/governance/`

## Success Criteria
- All 6 domains are assessed honestly
- Gaps are prioritized with clear rationale
- Action plan connects to specific CoE persona deliverables
- FDA/regulatory perspective is incorporated
