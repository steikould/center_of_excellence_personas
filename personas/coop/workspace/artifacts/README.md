## Living Documents

Some artifacts grow throughout your rotation:
- **Glossary** — Add terms every time you encounter something new
- **"Who Does What"** — Update as you meet people and learn about roles
- **Shadowing summaries** — Add entries each time you study a new domain

## What Goes to the Knowledge Base

Your work belongs in the shared knowledge base too! Consider contributing:
- Your **glossary** to `knowledge-base/best-practices/by-role/` (as a co-op onboarding resource)
- Your **gap analysis** observations to `knowledge-base/roadmap/`
- Your **process observations** to `knowledge-base/best-practices/by-domain/`
- Any **new schema proposals** to `knowledge-base/_schema/`

Your contributions are just as valid as anyone else's. The knowledge base doesn't care about your title — it cares about the quality of the knowledge.
