# Project Manager — Learning Journey

> Your path through the AI Center of Excellence. 8 shared modules to build Copilot mastery, then 4 role-specific modules to build your AI project management toolkit.

## How This Works

Each module has a prompt file that walks you through a hands-on challenge. Complete the challenge, save your work, and check the box. Your outputs accumulate in `personas/project-manager/workspace/` — by the end, you'll have a complete AI project management toolkit.

**Your agent**: `@project-manager` — invoke it anytime for role-specific guidance.

## Tips for Project Managers

- **Module 04 (Prompt Engineering)** is your power module. Build prompts for: status reports, risk summaries, meeting agendas, retrospective facilitation, and stakeholder briefings.
- **Module 05 (Agent Design)** — consider building a "project health dashboard" agent that reads your risk register and milestone tracker to generate status summaries.
- **Module 07 (Integration Patterns)** — map the systems your projects touch. Understanding data flows helps you estimate integration timelines accurately.
- **Your capstone** (Module 08) produces a reusable **AI Project Playbook** — templates and frameworks you'll use on every future AI initiative.
